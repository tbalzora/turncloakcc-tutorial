Welcome to Turncloak Card Conspiracy

Turncloak Card Conspiracy is a thrilling social deduction roguelike game where deception and strategy collide. In this game, you find yourself in a mysterious setting - whether it's a bustling casino, a quiet town, a ship at sea, or a space station - surrounded by a crew of characters.

How the Game Works: Each game round doesn't use all 18 cards at once. Instead, you'll play one of four game modes with different card counts and traitor configurations:

  • Mode 1 - 6-Card Classic: 5 Good cards, 1 Evil traitor (hidden)
  • Mode 2 - 8-Card Table: 7 Good cards, 1 Evil traitor (hidden)
  • Mode 3 - 10-Card Casino Heist: 8 Good cards, 2 Evil traitors (one hidden, one face-up disguised)
  • Mode 4 - 12-Card Grand Tower: 10 Good cards, 2 Evil traitors (both hidden, one face-up is secretly Evil)

The bad cards disguise themselves as good cards, making them nearly indistinguishable at first glance. Your mission is to sniff out the traitors through careful observation, deduction, and the classic "he said, she said, they said" gameplay mechanics. Each card has unique functionality that can help or hinder your investigation.

How to Play

Game Modes & Setup

At the start of each game, you'll choose one of four game modes, each with different card counts and traitor configurations:

🎴 MODE 1 - 6-CARD CLASSIC (1 Traitor): 5 Good, 1 Evil (hidden). Goal: Find the 1 disguised Evil hiding among the 6 face-up cards. Best for: Fast rounds, beginners, mobile party play.

🎴 MODE 2 - 8-CARD TABLE (1 Traitor): 7 Good roles (face-up), 1 Evil role (hidden). More Good abilities create conflicting "truth webs." Analyst becomes stronger. Best for: Medium-length games, 5-7 players.

🎴 MODE 3 - 10-CARD CASINO HEIST (2 Traitors): 8 Good roles (face-up), 2 Evil roles (one hidden, one face-up disguised as Good). The two Evil roles coordinate contradictions and spread misinformation. Goal: Identify the face-up disguised Evil card. Best for: Deep logic, experienced players.

🎴 MODE 4 - 12-CARD GRAND TOWER (2 Traitors): 10 Good roles (face-up), 2 Evil roles (both hidden, one of the face-up "Good" cards is secretly Evil). Maximum chaos and strategy. Best for: Experienced players, long sessions, deep strategy groups.

Gameplay Mechanics

The core of Turncloak Card Conspiracy revolves around social deduction and information gathering. Each card has unique abilities that provide information about other cards or reveal clues about who might be the traitor.

Good cards will provide truthful information to help you identify the traitors. Bad cards, however, disguise themselves as good cards and will provide false or misleading information to protect themselves and confuse investigators.

The "he said, she said, they said" mechanic means that information from different cards can be contradictory. You must carefully analyze all the information, look for patterns and inconsistencies, and determine which cards are telling the truth and which are lying.

Key abilities to watch for: The Analyst always reports the correct number of evil cards alive. The Dealer can detect contradictions. The Bartender gathers information through conversations. Use these abilities strategically to piece together the truth.

Winning Conditions

Good Team Wins: Successfully identify and correctly accuse all bad cards (traitors) hiding among the group.

Bad Team Wins: Avoid detection and prevent the good team from correctly identifying them, or survive until the end of the game without being caught.

The game can be played in single-player mode where you investigate against AI-controlled cards, or in multiplayer mode where players take on different card roles and use their abilities to gather and share information.

General Strategies

  • Observe Patterns: Pay attention to which cards are active and when. Bad cards may avoid certain interactions.
  • Listen to Sounds: Each card produces unique audio cues. Familiarize yourself with the sounds to identify suspicious behavior.
  • Question Everything: Don't trust information at face value. Verify claims through multiple sources.
  • Build Alliances: Work with other players to share information, but be wary - bad cards may try to infiltrate your group.
  • Use Card Abilities Wisely: Each card's ability is limited. Use them strategically to maximize their effectiveness.
